# Why Does Shopify Charge an Extra 2% Fee in India? (2026 Explained)

**Short answer:** Shopify Payments, Shopify's own payment system, is not available to stores in India. Indian stores must use a third-party gateway such as Razorpay, PayU or Cashfree. When you use a third-party gateway, Shopify charges its own transaction fee on each sale: 2% on the Basic plan. You pay that **on top of** the gateway's fee. So on Basic, a card or UPI sale can cost about 2% to Shopify plus the gateway's own fee (around 2% + GST at Razorpay's standard rate).

Figures in this article were checked on 10 November 2026 on Shopify's and Razorpay's own websites.

---

## The two fees, explained simply

### Fee 1: the payment gateway

Every online payment goes through a gateway (Razorpay, PayU, Cashfree, PhonePe, Paytm). The gateway charges for processing. Razorpay says its standard rate for Shopify stores is 2% plus 18% GST on domestic transactions, with no setup fee ([Razorpay](https://razorpay.com/blog/razorpay-shopify-payment-gateway-pricing-explained/)).

### Fee 2: Shopify's third-party transaction fee

Shopify's pricing page lists a **2% third-party transaction fee on Basic** ([Shopify pricing](https://www.shopify.com/pricing)). Higher plans have lower rates. This fee applies when you do *not* use Shopify Payments, and Shopify Payments is not offered to India-based stores ([doola](https://www.doola.com/blog/which-shopify-payment-gateways-can-indians-use/)).

Cash on Delivery orders are manual payments, so the online gateway fee does not apply to them. Check Shopify's current terms for how manual payment methods are treated on your plan.

---

## Worked example: ₹1,00,000 online sales a month on Shopify Basic

- Shopify third-party transaction fee: 2% = **₹2,000**
- Razorpay standard fee: 2% = ₹2,000, plus 18% GST = **₹2,360**
- Total fees: about **₹4,360 a month**, plus the Basic subscription
- Shopify lists Basic at **$29/month billed yearly, or $39 billed monthly**, in US dollars ([Shopify pricing](https://www.shopify.com/pricing))

So on ₹12 lakh of yearly online sales, the Shopify fee alone is about ₹24,000 a year, before the gateway fee and subscription.

Your real numbers depend on your plan, your gateway's rate (UPI rates may differ), and how many orders are COD.
