# Instamojo Alternative with Lower Fees in India (2026)

**Short answer:** Instamojo's own support page lists a transaction fee of **5% + ₹3** on Lite/Starter plans and **2% + ₹3** on Growth, plus 18% GST on the fee. On a ₹1,000 sale, that is about ₹63 on Starter. DigitalDukandar charges no per-order commission. You connect your own payment gateway and pay only its fee (about ₹23.60 on ₹1,000 at Razorpay's standard 2% + GST). Instamojo is still a good choice if you only need payment links for one or two digital products.

All figures were checked on 10 November 2026. Instamojo's different pages show different plan prices, so check the live pricing page before you decide.

---

## What Instamojo charges (from Instamojo's own pages)

**Transaction fee** ([Instamojo support](https://support.instamojo.com/hc/en-us/articles/208482245-Instamojo-Transaction-Fees-No-Setup-or-Maintenance-Fees)):

- Lite/Starter plans: 5% of the transaction + ₹3
- Growth plan: 2% of the transaction + ₹3
- 18% GST is charged on the fee
- Digital products may have higher rates; Instamojo's pages disagree on the exact figure
- No setup or maintenance fee

**Plans:** Lite is free. Paid plans are listed yearly on [Instamojo pricing](https://www.instamojo.com/pricing/).

Instamojo is itself a payment gateway, so its transaction fee covers payment processing.

## Worked example: one ₹1,000 order

**Instamojo Starter:**
- 5% of ₹1,000 = ₹50, + ₹3 = ₹53
- 18% GST on ₹53 = ₹9.54
- Total fee: **about ₹62.54**

**Instamojo Growth:**
- 2% of ₹1,000 = ₹20, + ₹3 = ₹23
- 18% GST on ₹23 = ₹4.14
- Total fee: **about ₹27.14**, plus the Growth subscription

**DigitalDukandar, with Razorpay at its standard rate:**
- DigitalDukandar commission: none
- Razorpay: 2% of ₹1,000 = ₹20, + 18% GST = ₹23.60 ([Razorpay](https://razorpay.com/blog/razorpay-shopify-payment-gateway-pricing-explained/))
- Total fee: **about ₹23.60**, plus your DigitalDukandar plan (free for the first year on the Free plan; [paid plan prices](https://digitaldukandar.in/pricing))
- On COD orders: no gateway fee

**At 100 orders of ₹1,000 a month:** Instamojo Starter fees come to about ₹6,254. With DigitalDukandar, you pay about ₹2,360 in Razorpay fees if every order is paid online, plus your plan's subscription.

Your real numbers depend on your gateway's rate (UPI rates may differ), your plan and how many orders are COD.
