# How WhatsApp Ordering Works on an Online Store — Step by Step (2026)

**Short answer:** The customer shops on your website like any online store. At checkout, the order is saved in your dashboard and WhatsApp opens on the customer's phone with the full order details (products, quantities, total and address) already typed and addressed to your number. The customer taps send, and you confirm the order in that chat. With DigitalDukandar, this works with a normal WhatsApp or WhatsApp Business number. No WhatsApp Business API is needed.

This article describes how WhatsApp ordering works on DigitalDukandar as of 10 November 2026.

---

## The customer's side, step by step

1. **Opens your store link**, for example from your Instagram bio or WhatsApp status.
2. **Browses products** and picks a size or variant if there are options.
3. **Adds to cart** and goes to checkout.
4. **Enters name, phone and address.**
5. **Chooses how to pay:** Cash on Delivery, or online (UPI, cards) through your payment gateway.
6. **Places the order.**
   - For COD, the button reads **Place Order via WhatsApp**.
   - For online payment, the customer pays first.
7. **WhatsApp opens** with the order message ready to send to your number. On Android and iPhone, the WhatsApp app opens directly; on a computer, WhatsApp Web opens in a new tab.
8. **Taps send.** The order is now in your WhatsApp chat.

If WhatsApp does not open by itself (some browsers block it), the page shows a button the customer can tap to open it.

---

## The seller's side

- **In your dashboard:** the order is saved with an order number, items, amount, payment method and address. You manage its status from the Orders page.
- **In WhatsApp:** you receive the customer's message with the same details. Reply to confirm, share delivery timing or ask for anything missing.
- **Stock:** if you use stock management, stock is tracked so sold-out items are not ordered again.
- **Shipping:** you can book a courier through the Shiprocket integration, or deliver yourself.

---

## Why this beats taking orders in chat only

- The customer sees prices and stock themselves, so you stop answering "price?" all day.
- Every order arrives complete, with address and quantities, in the same format.
- Every order is also saved in the dashboard, so nothing is lost in old chats.
- COD orders come with a chat already open, so confirming before shipping is quick. Shipping companies recommend confirming COD orders to reduce returns ([iCarry RTO guide](https://www.icarry.in/pages/blog/instagram-shipping-guide-cut-rto-social-sellers.html)).

## Setting it up

1. [Create your store](https://digitaldukandar.in/auth) and add products.
2. Add your WhatsApp number in store settings. Without it, the customer sees a message that WhatsApp ordering is not set up.
3. Choose COD, online payment, or both in payment settings.
4. Place a test order from your own phone to see exactly what your customers will see.

## Common problems and fixes

### WhatsApp does not open after the order

Usually the browser blocked the redirect. The customer can tap the button on the order page to open WhatsApp. Also check that your WhatsApp number in settings includes the country code.

### The customer placed the order but did not send the message

The order is still saved in your dashboard with their phone number, so you can message them first.

### I get too many fake COD orders

Confirm on WhatsApp before packing. If it continues, switch to online payment only, or offer a discount for paying online.

## Frequently asked questions

### Do I need the WhatsApp Business API?

No. The order message is sent from the customer's WhatsApp to your number. Your normal WhatsApp or WhatsApp Business app works.

### Is there a charge per WhatsApp message?

DigitalDukandar does not charge per message. The customer sends the message from their own WhatsApp.

### Can I see all WhatsApp orders in one place?

Yes. Every order is saved in your Orders dashboard as well as arriving in WhatsApp.
