# Dukaan Alternative in India — No Per-Order Service Fee (2026)

**Short answer:** Dukaan is a good, easy platform, but it charges a service fee on every order: 4.99% on its Free plan down to 0.99% on Infinity, capped at ₹200 per order. If that fee is why you are looking for an alternative, DigitalDukandar charges no per-order commission on any plan. The Free plan is free for the first year; paid plan prices are on the [pricing page](https://digitaldukandar.in/pricing). Each plan has a product limit, so very large catalogues may be better on Dukaan's higher plans.

All figures were checked on 10 November 2026 on Dukaan's and DigitalDukandar's own websites. Dukaan's older pages show different plan names and prices, so always check the live page.

## What Dukaan charges (from Dukaan's own help centre)

**Service fee per order** ([Dukaan help centre](https://help.mydukaan.io/article/1014-dukaan-service-fee-payment-gateway-charges)):

- Free plan: 4.99%
- Starter: 3.99%
- Growth: 1.99%
- Infinity: 0.99%
- Capped at ₹200 per order
- Manual orders you create yourself are exempt

**Plan prices**, listed yearly on [Dukaan's homepage](https://mydukaan.io/): Starter ₹1,999/year, Growth ₹5,999/year, Infinity ₹39,999/year, plus a Free plan.

## Worked example: ₹50,000 sales a month

Say you get 100 orders of ₹500 each.

**Dukaan Starter:**
- Service fee: 3.99% of ₹50,000 = ₹1,995
- Subscription: ₹1,999 ÷ 12 = about ₹167
- Total: about **₹2,162 a month**, before any payment gateway charges listed on Dukaan's help page

**Dukaan Growth:**
- Service fee: 1.99% of ₹50,000 = ₹995
- Subscription: ₹5,999 ÷ 12 = about ₹500
- Total: about **₹1,495 a month**, before gateway charges

**DigitalDukandar:**
- Commission: none
- Subscription: your plan's monthly price ([see pricing](https://digitaldukandar.in/pricing))
- Plus your own gateway's fee on online payments (no gateway fee on COD orders)

The gateway fee applies on both platforms when customers pay online, so the real difference is the service fee.
