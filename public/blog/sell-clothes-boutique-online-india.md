## What a clothing store needs that other stores don't

### Sizes and colours with their own stock

A kurti in size M may be sold out while L is still available. Your store must track stock **per size**, or customers will order sizes you don't have. In DigitalDukandar, each product can have variants (for example S, M, L, XL), and each variant has its own price, offer price, SKU and stock.

### Videos, not just photos

Fabric, fall and colour are hard to judge from a photo. A short video on the product page answers the questions customers would otherwise ask in DMs. DigitalDukandar supports product videos on every plan.

### Clear size and return policy

Most clothing returns happen because of fit. Put a size chart in each product description and a clear return/exchange policy on your store's policy page.

### COD, with a confirmation step

Many buyers want COD for clothes from a new boutique. To avoid parcels coming back (RTO), confirm each COD order on WhatsApp before you pack it. DigitalDukandar's COD orders arrive on your WhatsApp, so you can confirm in the same chat.

### Offers for festivals and new collections

Coupon codes and automatic discounts help with Diwali sales, wedding-season offers and influencer codes.

## Frequently asked questions

### Can I add sizes and colours to one product?

Yes. Add variants to a product, each with its own price, offer price, SKU and stock.

### Can I sell sarees and kurtis without GST?

GST rules depend on your turnover and how you sell. Check with a CA before you start. If you are GST-registered, DigitalDukandar lets you add your GSTIN and GST rate to checkout.

### How do I stop COD orders from coming back?

Confirm every COD order on WhatsApp before packing. If returns stay high, offer a discount for online payment or switch to online payment only.

### Is there a commission on clothing sales?

No. DigitalDukandar does not take a commission on any plan.
