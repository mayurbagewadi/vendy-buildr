# Online Store Builder with UPI, COD and Custom Domain in India (2026)

**Short answer:** You need three things from one platform: UPI payments into your own account, Cash on Delivery, and the option to use your own domain (like yourbrand.in). DigitalDukandar has all three. UPI and COD work on every plan. A custom domain needs the PRO plan. There is no per-order commission.

Prices and limits in this article were checked on 10 November 2026.

## UPI payments: what to check

UPI is how most Indian customers pay online. Before choosing a platform, check:

- **Whose account receives the money?** With DigitalDukandar, you connect your own payment gateway account (Razorpay, PhonePe, Cashfree, PayU or Paytm). Payments go to that account, not to DigitalDukandar.
- **Does the platform add a fee on top of the gateway?** DigitalDukandar does not. Shopify, for comparison, adds 2% per sale on its Basic plan when you use a third-party gateway, which all Indian stores must do ([Shopify pricing](https://www.shopify.com/pricing)).
- **What does the gateway itself charge for UPI?** Sources disagree on UPI charges. Some say UPI is free for merchants; Razorpay's own blog describes a platform fee on UPI ([Razorpay](https://razorpay.com/blog/razorpay-shopify-payment-gateway-pricing-explained/)). Ask your gateway for its current UPI rate before you sign up.

## Cash on Delivery: what to check

- **Can you turn COD on and off?** In DigitalDukandar's payment settings, you choose "online and COD" or "online only".
- **Does COD have a confirmation step?** COD orders on DigitalDukandar arrive on your WhatsApp, so you can confirm with the buyer before you pack. This is the most common advice for reducing returns (RTO).
- **Can you charge for delivery?** You can set a flat delivery fee, fee slabs by order value, or free delivery above an amount.

## Custom domain: what to check

A custom domain (yourbrand.in instead of yourbrand.digitaldukandar.in) makes your store look established and is easier to remember.

- **Which plan includes it?** On DigitalDukandar, custom domains are part of the PRO plan ([see current price](https://digitaldukandar.in/pricing)).
- **Do you buy the domain separately?** Yes. You buy the domain from any domain registrar and then connect it to your store.
- **Can you start without one?** Yes. Every store gets a free subdomain (yourstore.digitaldukandar.in). You can add your own domain later without rebuilding your store.

## Frequently asked questions

### Can I accept UPI on the free plan?

Yes. Connect your own gateway account. Monthly order limits for each plan are on the [pricing page](https://digitaldukandar.in/pricing).

### Do I need a GST number to accept UPI payments?

Payment gateways decide this during their own verification (KYC). Requirements differ between gateways, so check with the gateway you choose.

### Can I use a .in domain?

Yes. Any domain you own can be connected on the PRO plan.

### Does DigitalDukandar take a cut of UPI payments?

No. There is no commission on any plan. Only your gateway's fee applies.
