## The two costs every platform has

1. **Subscription:** what you pay monthly or yearly for the platform.
2. **Per-order fee:** a percentage the platform takes from each sale. Some call it a "transaction fee", "service fee" or "commission".

On top of both, your **payment gateway** (Razorpay, PhonePe, Cashfree, PayU, Paytm) charges for online payments. Cash on Delivery orders do not have a gateway fee.

### Instamojo

- Transaction fee: 5% + ₹3 per transaction on Lite/Starter, 2% + ₹3 on Growth, plus 18% GST on the fee ([Instamojo support](https://support.instamojo.com/hc/en-us/articles/208482245-Instamojo-Transaction-Fees-No-Setup-or-Maintenance-Fees))

### Shopify

- Basic is listed at $29/month billed yearly, or $39 billed monthly, in US dollars ([Shopify pricing](https://www.shopify.com/pricing)). That is well above ₹500/month.
- Shopify Payments is not available in India, so Indian stores use a third-party gateway, and Shopify then adds 2% per sale on Basic.

## Worked example: ₹30,000 sales a month

Say you sell 60 orders of ₹500 each in a month, all paid online.

**DigitalDukandar:** no commission, so you pay your plan's subscription ([see pricing](https://digitaldukandar.in/pricing)) plus your gateway's fee. At Razorpay's standard rate of 2% + GST ([Razorpay](https://razorpay.com/blog/razorpay-shopify-payment-gateway-pricing-explained/)), that is about ₹708. If those orders were COD instead, there would be no gateway fee.

**Dukaan Starter:** about ₹167/month (₹1,999 ÷ 12) + 3.99% service fee on ₹30,000 = ₹1,197, so about **₹1,364**, before any payment gateway charges Dukaan lists on the same help page.

**Instamojo Starter:** 5% + ₹3 on each of 60 orders = ₹1,680, plus 18% GST = about ₹1,982, plus the Starter subscription.

These are estimates from each company's published rates. Your real cost depends on your plan, your order sizes, and how many orders are COD.
