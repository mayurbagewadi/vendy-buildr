## Do Instagram sellers really need a website?

You can sell through DMs alone. Many sellers do. But as orders grow, DMs cause problems:

- You answer "price?" and "COD available?" dozens of times a day.
- Orders get lost between hundreds of chats.
- There is no record of stock, so you sell items you no longer have.

There is also a payment reason. According to [this 2026 guide to Instagram Shop in India](https://hodmedia.in/instagram-shop-india), Instagram has no in-app checkout in India. Product tags send buyers to a website to pay. Without a website, the order has to happen in DMs.

## What an Instagram seller's website must have

### A link that works from your bio

Your store link (for example yourstore.digitaldukandar.in) goes in your bio, stories and reels captions. The customer taps it and sees your products with prices and stock.

### Cash on Delivery, with an option to switch it off

Many Instagram buyers want COD because they have never bought from you before. But COD also brings RTO (return to origin): the buyer refuses the parcel and you pay shipping both ways. You need COD switched on at the start, and the option to switch to online-only payment if returns become a problem.

### UPI and card payments into your own account

Online payments should go to your own payment gateway account (Razorpay, PhonePe, Cashfree, PayU or Paytm), not to the platform first.

### Orders on WhatsApp

Many buyers who find you on Instagram still want to talk before buying. Orders that arrive on WhatsApp let you confirm quickly.

## How to reduce COD returns (RTO)

Shipping companies that serve Instagram sellers give the same advice: **confirm every COD order by call or WhatsApp before you pack it** ([iCarry RTO guide](https://www.icarry.in/pages/blog/instagram-shipping-guide-cut-rto-social-sellers.html)). A buyer who does not reply probably will not accept the parcel.

With DigitalDukandar, COD orders already arrive on your WhatsApp as a message from the customer, so you can reply and confirm in the same chat. Other tips:

- Offer a small discount for online payment, using coupon codes or automatic discounts.
- Set a delivery fee, or free delivery above a minimum order, so small risky orders cost less to lose.
- If returns stay high, switch your store to online payment only in your payment settings.
