## Why WhatsApp sellers need a store, not just a catalogue

Selling only through chats works until orders grow. Then the same problems show up:

- Customers ask "price?" and "available?" for every product, again and again.
- Order details (size, quantity, address) are scattered across chats.
- You cannot easily see what sold this month or what is out of stock.
- Payments come in as screenshots that you have to match by hand.

A store fixes this. Customers see prices, stock and photos themselves, and each order reaches you complete.

## 5 things to check before you choose a builder

### 1. Does the order arrive on your WhatsApp with full details?

Some builders only add a "chat with us" button. That is not an order system. You want the customer's name, phone, address, products, quantities and total to reach your WhatsApp in one message, and the same order saved in a dashboard.

### 2. Is Cash on Delivery supported, and can you switch it off?

Many Indian buyers still prefer COD. But COD also brings returns when buyers refuse the parcel. You want a builder that lets you offer COD *and* lets you turn it off (online payment only) if returns become a problem.

### 3. Does online payment go into your own account?

Check whether UPI and card payments go to your own payment gateway account (Razorpay, PhonePe, Cashfree, PayU or Paytm), or whether the platform collects the money first and pays you later.

### 4. Is there a commission per order?

This is the most expensive hidden cost. Some platforms charge a percentage of every sale on top of the payment gateway fee. For example, Dukaan's help centre lists a service fee of 0.99% to 4.99% per order depending on the plan, capped at ₹200 per order ([Dukaan help centre](https://help.mydukaan.io/article/1014-dukaan-service-fee-payment-gateway-charges)). Shopify adds 2% on its Basic plan when you use a third-party gateway, which every Indian store must do ([Shopify pricing](https://www.shopify.com/pricing)).

### 5. Can you manage stock and orders in one place?

As you grow, you need stock counts, order status and a list of past customers. Without these, you are back to scrolling chats.
